How to play

Play a card that matches the discard pile's color or number, or play a Wild. If you cannot play, draw a card. First player to empty their hand wins.

  • Skip: the next player misses a turn.
  • Reverse: reverses the direction of play.
  • Draw Two: the next player draws two and misses their turn.
  • Wild: choose the color to continue.
  • Wild Draw Four: choose a color; next player draws four and misses their turn. Official rules restrict this to when you have no card matching the current color.
  • UNO: when you have one card, press UNO! to call it out. This starter version does not automatically penalize missed calls.
